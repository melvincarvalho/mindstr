    Save your map

    Mindstr saves maps to a pod: storage you control on the web, so the map stays yours and you can share a link to it.

    Your map is saved in public/mindstr/ in your pod, where anyone with the link can view it. Only you can change it.

    Sign in with a Nostr browser extension (such as nos2x or Alby) or with your Solid provider. You only need to paste a key if you know what you're doing.

    On a phone, choose the Solid tab and sign in with your Solid provider.
